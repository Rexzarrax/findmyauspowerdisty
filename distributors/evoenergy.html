Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Evoenergy?
Use the outage link above, or call Evoenergy's outage line directly. Evoenergy is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Evoenergy the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Evoenergy owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in ACT — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Evoenergy.
What area does Evoenergy cover?
Evoenergy services the ACT suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Evoenergy directly for an exact address.
