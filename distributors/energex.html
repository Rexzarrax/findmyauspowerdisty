Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Energex?
Use the outage link above, or call Energex's outage line directly. Energex is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Energex the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Energex owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in QLD — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Energex.
What area does Energex cover?
Energex services the QLD suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Energex directly for an exact address.
