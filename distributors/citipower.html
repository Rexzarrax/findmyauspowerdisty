Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with CitiPower?
Use the outage link above, or call CitiPower's outage line directly. CitiPower is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is CitiPower the same as my electricity retailer?
No. CitiPower owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in VIC — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, CitiPower.
What area does CitiPower cover?
CitiPower services the VIC suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with CitiPower directly for an exact address.
