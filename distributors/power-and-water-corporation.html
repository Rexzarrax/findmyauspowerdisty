Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Power and Water Corporation?
Use the outage link above, or call Power and Water Corporation's outage line directly. Power and Water Corporation is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Power and Water Corporation the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Power and Water Corporation owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in NT — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Power and Water Corporation.
What area does Power and Water Corporation cover?
Power and Water Corporation services the NT suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Power and Water Corporation directly for an exact address.
