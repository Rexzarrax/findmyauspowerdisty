Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Essential Energy?
Use the outage link above, or call Essential Energy's outage line directly. Essential Energy is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Essential Energy the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Essential Energy owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in NSW — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Essential Energy.
What area does Essential Energy cover?
Essential Energy services the NSW suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Essential Energy directly for an exact address.
