Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Horizon Power?
Use the outage link above, or call Horizon Power's outage line directly. Horizon Power is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Horizon Power the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Horizon Power owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in WA — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Horizon Power.
What area does Horizon Power cover?
Horizon Power services the WA suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Horizon Power directly for an exact address.
