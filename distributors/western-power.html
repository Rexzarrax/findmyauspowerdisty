Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Western Power?
Use the outage link above, or call Western Power's outage line directly. Western Power is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Western Power the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Western Power owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in WA — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Western Power.
What area does Western Power cover?
Western Power services the WA suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Western Power directly for an exact address.
