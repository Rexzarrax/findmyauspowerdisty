Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Ergon Energy?
Use the outage link above, or call Ergon Energy's outage line directly. Ergon Energy is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Ergon Energy the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Ergon Energy owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in QLD — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Ergon Energy.
