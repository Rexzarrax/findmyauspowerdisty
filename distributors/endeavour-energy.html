Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Endeavour Energy?
Use the outage link above, or call Endeavour Energy's outage line directly. Endeavour Energy is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Endeavour Energy the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Endeavour Energy owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in NSW — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Endeavour Energy.
What area does Endeavour Energy cover?
