Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with SA Power Networks?
Use the outage link above, or call SA Power Networks's outage line directly. SA Power Networks is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is SA Power Networks the same as my electricity retailer?
No. SA Power Networks owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in SA — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, SA Power Networks.
What area does SA Power Networks cover?
