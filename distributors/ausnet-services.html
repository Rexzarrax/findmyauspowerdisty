Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with AusNet Services?
Use the outage link above, or call AusNet Services's outage line directly. AusNet Services is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is AusNet Services the same as my electricity retailer?
No. AusNet Services owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in VIC — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, AusNet Services.
What area does AusNet Services cover?
AusNet Services services the VIC suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with AusNet Services directly for an exact address.
