Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Ausgrid?
Use the outage link above, or call Ausgrid's outage line directly. Ausgrid is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Ausgrid the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Ausgrid owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in NSW — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Ausgrid.
What area does Ausgrid cover?
Ausgrid services the NSW suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Ausgrid directly for an exact address.
