Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with United Energy?
Use the outage link above, or call United Energy's outage line directly. United Energy is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is United Energy the same as my electricity retailer?
No. United Energy owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in VIC — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, United Energy.
What area does United Energy cover?
United Energy services the VIC suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with United Energy directly for an exact address.
