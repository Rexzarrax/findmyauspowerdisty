Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Jemena Electricity Networks?
Use the outage link above, or call Jemena Electricity Networks's outage line directly. Jemena Electricity Networks is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Jemena Electricity Networks the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Jemena Electricity Networks owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in VIC — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Jemena Electricity Networks.
What area does Jemena Electricity Networks cover?
Jemena Electricity Networks services the VIC suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with Jemena Electricity Networks directly for an exact address.
