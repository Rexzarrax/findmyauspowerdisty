Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with TasNetworks?
Use the outage link above, or call TasNetworks's outage line directly. TasNetworks is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is TasNetworks the same as my electricity retailer?
No. TasNetworks owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in TAS — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, TasNetworks.
What area does TasNetworks cover?
TasNetworks services the TAS suburbs and towns listed above. Boundaries shown on the map are approximate — always confirm with TasNetworks directly for an exact address.
