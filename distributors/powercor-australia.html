Frequently asked questions

How do I report a power outage with Powercor Australia?
Use the outage link above, or call Powercor Australia's outage line directly. Powercor Australia is the network operator (the poles and wires) — they handle outages and faults, not your electricity retailer/biller.
Is Powercor Australia the same as my electricity retailer?
No. Powercor Australia owns and maintains the physical power lines in this area. Your retailer (who sends your bill) can be any company operating in VIC — but outages and faults are always handled by the local distributor, Powercor Australia.
